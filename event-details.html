Technology Day

Date: 20 October 2026

Time: 10:00 AM - 2:00 PM

Venue: Main Hall

Technology Day is a campus event where students can discover new ideas, share projects, and learn about technology. Students can meet other people who are interested in computers and innovation.

Event Agenda

  • 10:00 AM - Welcome and opening.
  • 10:30 AM - Student project presentations.
  • 12:00 PM - Technology activity.
  • 1:00 PM - Questions and networking.
  • 2:00 PM - Event ends.

Entry Information

Entry is free for university students. Please register before attending and bring your student ID.
